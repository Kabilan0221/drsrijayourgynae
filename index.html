Dr. M. Srija is a qualified obstetrician and gynaecologist practising at Sri Sai Clinic & Scans in Konganapuram. She provides care across pregnancy, childbirth, gynaecological health and infertility, alongside general medicine consultations for the whole family.

The clinic is built around one idea — every mother should feel heard, cared for, and confident through her journey. From the first scan to postnatal recovery, care here is personal and unhurried.
